# Saleka platform notes

## Protected project delivery

Freelancers upload final project files from the project conversation. Files are stored privately and can be previewed by project participants inside Saleka. A buyer's direct download is enabled only after they accept the delivery; buyers can request a revision instead. In-app previews are an access control feature, not DRM, and cannot prevent screenshots or copying of visible content.

## Points and rewards

Freelancers receive 50 spendable points when a buyer accepts a final delivery. The dashboard shows the current redeemable balance and lifetime earned points. Lifetime milestones are 100, 200, 250, 500, 1,000, and 2,500 points; each eligible milestone gift can be claimed once. Store redemptions deduct spendable points, while milestone progress remains based on lifetime points. Delivery awards and redemptions are recorded in the points transaction log.

Fake deliveries, collusion, and manipulated ratings are not eligible for rewards and may be reviewed under the platform terms.
